Place the company logo in this folder as:

    logo.png

The app references it at:  images/logo.png
(configured in js/company.js -> COMPANY.logo)

Recommended: a PNG with a transparent or white background,
roughly 240px tall for crisp printing. SVG/JPG also work —
just update the filename in js/company.js to match.

If no logo file is present, the invoice gracefully falls back
to showing the company name only.
